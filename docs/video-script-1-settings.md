# Video 1 Script — "Set Up ohACCESS in 5 Minutes" (Settings walkthrough)

**Target length:** 4–5 minutes
**What you need before recording:** a clean test account with Settings already
half-filled (so you're typing into one or two fields, not all of them), your
headshot/logo URLs ready to paste, and your browser at 100% zoom.
**Format below:** `[SCREEN]` = what to show. `VO` = what you say (written to be
read aloud — read it naturally, don't rush).

---

## Scene 1 — Intro (0:00–0:20)

[SCREEN] Dashboard, then click the **Settings** tab.

VO: Hi, I'm Dave, founder of ohACCESS. Before your first open house, let's
spend five minutes in Settings. Everything you enter here shows up on your
printed QR sign and on every email your visitors get — so it all looks like
you and your brand, not like us. Let's go top to bottom.

## Scene 2 — Agent Profile (0:20–1:10)

[SCREEN] Scroll to **Agent Profile**. Fill in or point to each field as you mention it.

VO: First, your profile. Your name and brokerage — these appear on visitor
emails and your seller reports. Your phone number. The **Display Email** is
the address visitors will see on their emails — so if you want leads
writing to a specific inbox, put that one here. Add your license number
and state — some states expect it on anything visitor-facing, and we
display it for you automatically.

## Scene 3 — Branding (1:10–2:00)

[SCREEN] Scroll to **Branding**. Paste a headshot URL and logo URL, then change
a brand color and let the live preview update on screen.

VO: Now the fun part — branding. Add your headshot and your logo, and pick
your two brand colors. Watch the preview update as I change them. Your
logo and colors dress up the printed sign at the front door, and your
headshot appears on the emails every visitor receives. Thirty seconds of
setup, and every open house you ever run is branded like your business.

## Scene 4 — Send Leads to Your CRM (2:00–2:50)

[SCREEN] Scroll to **Send Leads to Your CRM**. Open the CRM dropdown so the
list is visible (Follow Up Boss, BoldTrail/kvCORE, Lofty, Sierra Interactive,
Real Geeks, CINC, Top Producer, Wise Agent, LionDesk…), pick one, and paste a
lead-intake email.

VO: If you work out of a CRM, don't skip this. Pick your CRM from the
list, paste in your lead-intake email address — that's the special address
your CRM gives you for importing leads — and from then on, every sign-in
lands in your CRM as a formatted lead, automatically. Name, phone, email,
and how soon they're buying. No exporting, no retyping. This works during
your free trial too.

## Scene 5 — The power features, quickly (2:50–4:00)

[SCREEN] Scroll slowly through **Your Own Questions**, **Disclosures &
Notices**, and **Agreements Signed Before Entry** — a few seconds each, no
clicking around.

VO: Below that are three features you can come back to later, but you
should know they exist. **Your Own Questions** lets you add your own
question to the sign-in form — like "How did you hear about this open
house?" — and up to two more after they finish. **Disclosures and
Notices** attaches your state-required disclosure links, and delivers them
to every visitor automatically — that's your agency disclosure on
autopilot. And **Agreements Signed Before Entry** lets you upload a
touring agreement as a PDF, and visitors sign it right on their phone
before they get their entry codewords. Signed copies are emailed to both
of you instantly.

## Scene 6 — Wrap (4:00–4:30)

[SCREEN] Scroll back to the top of Settings, then click the **New Open House** tab and pause on the empty form.

VO: That's it — your account is dressed and ready. In the next video, we'll
create your first open house, print your sign, and I'll show you what your
visitors see at the door. See you there.

---

*Cut-for-time note: if you want this under 4 minutes, Scene 5 is the one to
tighten — one sentence per feature is enough. Don't cut Scene 4; the CRM
hookup is the setting people miss most and then wish they'd done first.*
