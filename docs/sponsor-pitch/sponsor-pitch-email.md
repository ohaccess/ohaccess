**What the visitor sees**
The moment a visitor signs in, they get a codeword email and a text. Under the agent's card sits your "Sponsored by" card: your headshot, company, phone, email, licence or NMLS number, and a link to your details. The same card appears in the next-morning thank-you email.

**How you get the visitor data for all your sponsored agents' open houses**

1. **Set up your Sponsor Portal.** Create your account, add your headshot, logo and contact details. One account covers up to 10 agents.
2. **Invite your agents.** Enter each agent's email. They accept with one click and keep their own branding. Nothing shows on their open houses until they accept.
3. **Visitors sign in and consent.** At the door, visitors scan the agent's QR code. The sign-in page names you as today's sponsor, and the visitor agrees to be contacted by the agent and by you, by phone, text and email.
4. **Leads land in your dashboard as they happen.** Your portal shows every sponsored sign-in, grouped by agent and by open house, with totals for sign-ins and visitors verified at the door.
5. **Export any time.** One click on Export CSV downloads every lead, or just one open house, ready for Excel or your CRM.

**Each lead includes:** name, mobile number (verified by text), email, buying timeline, date and time, whether they were verified at the door, and which open house and agent they came from.

**Built to keep you compliant.** You only ever see visitors whose consent named you, and nothing from before a sponsorship began. Every email carries the "not required to use, free to shop around" notice. The consent language and labeling were reviewed by counsel for RESPA and TCPA.

**Pricing:** $120 a month for up to 10 agents, or $1,200 a year. The agents you sponsor get full Pro access at no cost to them, which makes you easy to say yes to.
