3 · How the visitor data reaches you
Nothing to install, nothing for the agent to export or forward. One sponsor account covers up to 10 agents.
1Invite your agentsCreate your Sponsor Portal account, add your headshot, logo and contact details, then invite each agent by email. They accept with one click and keep their own branding.
2Visitors sign in and consentAt the door, visitors scan the agent's QR code. The sign-in page names you as today's sponsor, and the visitor agrees to be contacted by the agent and by you.
3Your card goes out instantlyThe visitor's codeword email carries your “Sponsored by” card. Their phone is verified by text, and the agent confirms the codeword at the door.
4Leads land in your dashboardEvery sign-in appears in your portal as it happens, grouped by agent and by open house. Export a CSV any time and load it into your own CRM.
