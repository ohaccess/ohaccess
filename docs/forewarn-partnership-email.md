# FOREWARN partnership email (draft, 2026-10-06)

To: sales@forewarn.com
Subject: Integration request: ohACCESS open-house sign-in + FOREWARN

Hi FOREWARN team,

I'm Dave Sheehan, founder of ohACCESS (ohaccess.com). We're a verified
open-house sign-in service: visitors scan a QR code at the door, enter their
phone number and email, and we text them a codeword that proves the number is
real before they're let in. Agents get a verified phone number for every person
who walks through the door, often while they're alone in the house with them.

That makes our agents exactly the people FOREWARN is built for, at exactly the
moment they need it. Today they have to copy a visitor's number out of
ohACCESS and paste it into FOREWARN by hand. We'd like to close that gap.

Your product page mentions API and custom integrations, and we've seen the
embedded app you built with Follow Up Boss. We'd like to do the same: a
FOREWARN panel on the visitor's record in ohACCESS that, once the agent signs
in to FOREWARN, searches the visitor's phone number automatically. If an
embedded widget isn't the right fit, a link format that opens FOREWARN with a
number pre-filled would get us most of the way.

A few things that may matter to you:

- Every number we'd pass has already been verified by a one-time codeword, so
  searches are on real, reachable numbers.
- Our agents are mostly US REALTORS® who already have FOREWARN through their
  association or MLS. We would not resell or re-display FOREWARN data; the
  report stays inside your widget, under your terms and your login.
- We'd present the integration strictly as identity verification and personal
  safety, with the same fair-housing language your Follow Up Boss article uses.

Could you point me to the right person to talk about partner integrations?
I'm happy to share a demo of the agent flow and current volume.

Thanks,
Dave Sheehan
Founder, ohACCESS
