# ohACCESS — Product Facts for Article Writing (Source of Truth)

Use this document as the authoritative description of ohACCESS when writing or updating any article. Where anything conflicts with an earlier article or your prior understanding, THIS DOCUMENT WINS. Previous articles contained recurring factual errors, listed at the bottom — do not repeat them.

## What ohACCESS is

ohACCESS (www.ohaccess.com) replaces the paper open-house sign-in sheet with verified digital check-in. A visitor scans a QR code at the door, fills out a short registration form on their own phone, and instantly receives verification codewords. They share a codeword with the host agent to enter the property — which proves the phone number and email they entered are real and theirs. The agent gets an instant alert by text and email for every sign-in, plus a running verified visitor log.

## The verification flow — describe it EXACTLY like this

1. Visitor scans the QR code (printed sign at the door, or the agent's permanent reusable QR).
2. They fill out a short form on their own phone: name, email, phone, buying timeline (0–3 / 3–6 / 6–12 / 12+ months), plus up to one custom question the agent added. The form is available in 16 languages.
3. The moment they submit, ohACCESS sends **two codewords: one by text message and one by email** — both, always, immediately.
4. The visitor **tells a codeword to the host agent at the door** to get in. The text codeword is primary; the email codeword is the backup if the text is slow.
5. The agent instantly receives a text and email alert with the visitor's details.

Critical mechanics:
- The visitor **never types or enters a codeword into any app, screen, or system**. There is no "enter the code to unlock entry" step anywhere. The codeword is spoken/shown to the human host.
- It is always **two codewords** (text + email), never "a codeword via text or email."
- Codewords are set per open house by the agent (with an auto-generate button). They are **not** unique per visitor and they **never expire on a timer**.
- Visitors with non-US/Canada phone numbers receive their codeword via **WhatsApp**. International addresses and phones are fully supported.
- No app download, no account, nothing to install for visitors. Returning visitors are remembered for a year and see their info pre-filled.

## Pricing — published and flat; NEVER say "contact us for a quote"

- **Free trial:** the first **25 visitor registrations** are free, every feature included, no credit card. After 25, the account locks until the agent subscribes. This is a **free trial — NOT a "free plan" or "free tier."** There is no ongoing free plan.
- **Pro (solo agent):** $15/month, $150/year, or $240 for 2 years.
- **Team (2–10 agents):** flat $120/month.
- **Brokerage (11–100 agents):** $11 per agent per month, self-serve. 100+ agents = contact us.
- Pricing NEVER varies with how many open houses or visitors an agent has. No volume discounts, no usage-based pricing.

## CRM integration — NEVER say "native API sync"

ohACCESS delivers every sign-in into the agent's CRM automatically through exactly three mechanisms:
1. A **formatted lead email** sent to the CRM's lead-intake address, labeled so the CRM parses it into fields automatically (verified working with Follow Up Boss; agents pick their CRM from a dropdown — Follow Up Boss, BoldTrail/kvCORE, Lofty, Sierra Interactive, Real Geeks, CINC, Top Producer, Wise Agent, LionDesk, Other).
2. A **Zapier webhook** (connects to thousands of apps).
3. **Team CRM forwarding** (a team lead can copy every member's leads to a shared team CRM).

Do NOT claim: native/direct API integrations, two-way sync, CRM field mapping, automatic tagging, or lead scoring. None of those exist. Correct phrasing: "delivers leads into your CRM automatically" / "sends every sign-in to your CRM as a formatted lead" — not "syncs directly."

## The seller report — privacy-safe; NEVER say sellers see visitor details

Agents can share a one-tap Seller Report page with the home seller. It shows **aggregate data only**: registered-visitor count, how many plan to buy within 6 months, the buying-timeline breakdown, a scan-to-registration funnel, the average 1–10 visitor rating and price verdict (Too High / Reasonable / Too Low), and aggregated answers to the agent's custom questions.

It deliberately contains **NO visitor names, phone numbers, emails, arrival times, or per-visitor rows**. Visitors consented to the agent having their info, not the seller. This privacy design is a selling point — describe it as one. Never tell agents to include visitor contact info in a seller report.
