Why ohACCESS

Four reasons the clipboard has to go
1. Paper leads are bad leads
Illegible names. Made-up emails. Phone numbers off by a digit. With ohACCESS, visitors type their own details on their own phone, so names are legible, and the phone and email are proven real because that's where the codewords are sent.
2. You owe it to your sellers, and to yourself
An open house invites strangers into someone's home. The sign out front tells every visitor this is a verified environment. Bad actors prefer the path of least resistance, and a codeword at the door is often enough to make them walk away. Every sign-in also leaves a digital record: time, device, IP address, and phone carrier.
ohACCESS is a deterrence layer, not a substitute for your safety practices or brokerage protocols.
3. Your follow-up runs itself
While you greet buyers, ohACCESS builds your visitor log, sends every sign-in to your CRM as a formatted lead, thanks each visitor the next morning in your branding, and emails you a lead report grouped by buying timeline 30 minutes after you lock the door.
4. It wins you the next listing
Send your seller a one-tap Seller Report: how many people came, how many plan to buy within 6 months, the average visitor rating of the home, and whether visitors think the price is too high, reasonable, or too low. It never shows visitor names or contact details, so your leads stay yours.
How it works

Three minutes of setup. Nothing for visitors to download.
1
 Create your open house. Type the address and it fills in the city, state, zip, and time zone. Pick your date, times, and two codewords (or tap auto-generate).
2
 Print your branded QR sign and set it by the front door. Your permanent QR code works for every open house, so you can laminate one sign and reuse it forever.
3
 Visitors scan and sign in on their own phone. Name, email, phone, and buying timeline. The instant they tap the button, they get two codewords: one by text and one by email. You get a text and email alert with their details.
4
 They tell you a codeword at the door. Fake phone or email means no codeword, and no codeword means no entry. Everything after that happens automatically.
