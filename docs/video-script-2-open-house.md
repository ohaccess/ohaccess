# Video 2 Script — "Your First Open House" (create, run, and the hidden features)

**Target length:** 6–7 minutes
**What you need before recording:** your test account with Settings done
(so branding shows up), one open house already created *with a few test
visitors in the log* (so the log and Invite scenes aren't empty), and a
phone handy for the visitor segment. Use your own phone number for the test
sign-in so no real visitor data appears on screen.
**Format:** `[SCREEN]` = what to show. `VO` = what you say.

---

## Scene 1 — Intro (0:00–0:15)

[SCREEN] Dashboard, empty state: "No open houses yet. Create your first one!"

VO: Let's create your first open house — start to finish, it's about three
minutes — and then I'll show you everything ohACCESS does around it
automatically, plus a few features most people don't find on day one.

## Scene 2 — The form (0:15–1:15)

[SCREEN] Click **New Open House**. Start typing a street address and let the
autocomplete fill city, state, and zip. Pick a date on the calendar, set
start and end times. Fill price, beds, baths quickly.

VO: Click New Open House. Start typing the address and pick it from the
list — city, state, zip, even the property's time zone fill in on their
own. Choose your date and your start and end times. Price, square footage,
beds and baths are optional, but fill them in — they appear in the email
your visitors get, and on your seller report. Drop in the listing link if
you have one.

## Scene 3 — Codewords (1:15–2:00)

[SCREEN] Scroll to **Codewords**. Click auto-generate on each —
let words like STUNNING and VILLA pop in.

VO: Now, the heart of ohACCESS: the codewords. When a visitor signs in,
we instantly text them one codeword and email them another. To get into
your open house, they show you a code — and that's the trick: if they can
show you the texted code, that phone number is real and in their hand. No
more fake numbers on a paper sheet. At the door, always ask for the *text*
code first — the email code is the backup. Create your own branded or
unique codewords, or tap auto-generate and let us pick for you. Hit save.

## Scene 4 — Your sign and your QR codes (2:00–3:00)

[SCREEN] On the new card, click **📱 QR Code**. Show the printable sign.
Then close and hover **📌 My QR code** at the top of the dashboard.

VO: Your open house now has its own QR code. Click QR Code and print the
sign — it's already carrying your logo and colors. Notice there's no
address on it. That's on purpose: laminate this once and reuse it at every
open house you ever hold. Even better — see **My QR code** at the top?
That's your permanent code. It automatically points to whichever open
house you're holding next. Print it once, stick it on your sign stand, and
never print another.

## Scene 5 — What your visitor sees (3:00–4:00)

[SCREEN] Phone segment. Scan the sign with your phone: show the branded
form, tap the flag picker briefly, submit a test sign-in with your own
number. Show the text arriving, then show the alert hitting *your* email.

VO: Here's the visitor's side. They scan, and get a thirty-second form —
your branding, their language; it speaks sixteen. The moment they submit,
their codeword arrives by text, and a second one by email. You get an
alert the instant each visitor signs in. And if they've ever signed into
any ohACCESS open house before, the form recognizes them and fills itself
out — repeat visitors are in, in about ten seconds.

## Scene 6 — The visitor log (4:00–4:45)

[SCREEN] Back on the dashboard, open the open house with test visitors.
Point at a row: tap the verify toggle, show the timeline pill, open a
visitor and type a short private note.

VO: Every sign-in lands here, live. When someone shows you their codeword
at the door, tap once — verified. The colored pill is their buying
timeline — orange means buying within three months, your hottest leads.
Click any visitor for tap-to-call and tap-to-email, and a private notes
field — "pre-approved, wants three bedrooms, follow up Monday." If a
number is a burner app or a text bounced, a warning badge appears on the
row — the fakes expose themselves.

## Scene 7 — What happens automatically (4:45–5:30)

[SCREEN] Show the reminder email in your inbox (pre-stage this), scroll it
briefly — the sign links, the door scripts. Then flash the post-event
report email.

VO: From here, ohACCESS runs the event with you. The day before, you get
this reminder — your sign, a placement tip, and two door scripts you can
read word-for-word, including a friendly answer for the visitor who
hesitates to register. The next morning, every visitor gets a thank-you
email with your photo and contact info — you're following up while your
competition is still typing names off a paper sheet. And about half an
hour after you close, this report: every lead, grouped by how soon
they're buying.

## Scene 8 — The seller report (5:30–6:00)

[SCREEN] Click **📊 Seller report**. Scroll the report page slowly.

VO: This one's for your listing client. One tap makes a shareable results
page — how many came through, how soon they're buying, how they rated the
home and the price. Notice what's *not* on it: no names, no phone numbers.
Your leads stay yours. Text the link to your seller Sunday night and be
the agent with data.

## Scene 9 — 💌 Invite: fill your next open house (6:00–6:45)

[SCREEN] Click **💌 Invite** on an upcoming open house. Show the checkbox
list of eligible past visitors and the "not included" breakdown. Send (or
stop just short if it's all test data).

VO: And here's the feature that pays for the whole thing. When you
schedule a new open house, tap Invite. ohACCESS looks back through
everyone who's ever signed into one of your open houses, finds the people
still inside the buying window they told you about, and emails them a
personal invitation — your name, the address, an RSVP link. It's
deliberately polite: nobody gets more than two invites a month, and
opt-outs are always excluded — you can see exactly who's included and why
before anything sends. Every open house you hold builds the audience for
your next one.

## Scene 10 — Wrap (6:45–7:00)

[SCREEN] Hover the **⧉ Duplicate** button, then pull back to the full dashboard.

VO: One last trick — holding the same property open next weekend? Duplicate.
Two clicks, pick the new date, done. Your first twenty-five sign-ins are
free — that's one or two good open houses. Go schedule one, and put the
paper sheet in the recycling.

---

*Cut-for-time notes: Scenes 1–4 plus 9 are the core — if you want a shorter
cut, trim Scenes 6 and 7 to a sentence or two each. Keep Scene 9 (Invite)
at full length; it's the feature nobody discovers on their own and the
strongest reason to stay past the free trial. If staging the reminder email
for Scene 7 is a hassle, you can narrate it over the dashboard instead and
cut 20 seconds.*
